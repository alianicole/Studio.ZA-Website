Charles Mews

Chelsea, London 2024 Full interior design

A four-storey mews house in the heart of Chelsea, acquired by a young family who wanted it to feel like it had always been theirs — none of the show-home stiffness that so often plagues London properties at this price point.

We stripped the interior back to its Georgian bones: exposed the original timber joists on the ground floor, uncovered a fireplace that had been bricked up in the 1970s, and rebuilt the staircase with slim steel balusters that feel contemporary without shouting about it.

The palette is drawn from the mews cobblestones themselves — warm greys, worn ochres, and the particular dusty rose of old London brick. Against this, we introduced pattern through textiles: a hand-blocked linen in the drawing room, a Neisha Crosland print in the principal bathroom, an antique dhurrie on the kitchen floor that anchors the room better than any rug we could have sourced new.

The clients wanted somewhere genuinely comfortable for their children — not a place that would need to be put away when they came home from school. So the sofas are deep and slipcovered in a washable linen, the kitchen table is scrubbed oak that will only get better with use, and the mudroom off the back gate has hooks at three different heights.
