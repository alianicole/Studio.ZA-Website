About

Rooms that earn their keep.

Studio.ZA was founded by Zoe Adden in 2015 with a simple conviction: that the best interior design is never really about the interior. It's about the people who live inside it — the way they move, gather, retreat, cook, argue, celebrate.

We work primarily in London and the English countryside, on private houses and apartments where the brief is to make a home feel completely, unapologetically itself. That might mean commissioning a bespoke kitchen from a joiner in Suffolk, or it might mean sourcing a 19th-century chandelier from a dealer in Lyon. We're equally happy doing both.

The studio is deliberately small — Zoe leads every project personally, supported by a tight team of designers and project coordinators. We take on a limited number of commissions each year so that each one gets the attention it deserves.

Philosophy

Against the tyranny of the impractical.

We believe in fabrics that can be washed, surfaces that get better with age, and sofas deep enough to actually fall asleep on. We believe in colour used with confidence, pattern used without apology, and furniture that tells a story — whether it was made last year or a hundred years ago.

We are suspicious of spaces that look finished. The best rooms always have a little more to discover: a book spine that prompts a question, a chair that turns out to be more comfortable than it looks, a view you didn't notice the first time. We aim to design those rooms.

Above all, we believe that a well-designed home is one of the most profound acts of care a person can make — for their family, their friends, themselves. That's the standard we hold ourselves to, every time.

How we work

A process built around listening.

01

Discovery

We meet at your home — existing or prospective — and spend time understanding how you actually live. No questionnaires, just conversation.

02

Concept

We present a single, considered design direction. Not three options to hedge our bets — one, because we've done the thinking and we stand behind it.

03

Sourcing

We draw on two decades of supplier relationships — from established makers to the small workshops we've discovered along the way — to find pieces that are genuinely right.

04

Delivery

We manage the installation ourselves. On handover day, the flowers are already in the vase. You walk in and it's home.
