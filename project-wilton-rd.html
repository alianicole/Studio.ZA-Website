Wilton Road

Belgravia, London 2023 Full interior design

The brief: a Victorian terrace that feels genuinely easy to live in, without any of its character lost. White-painted exposed brick runs through the ground floor; a three-arm Serge Mouille ceiling light anchors the reception room; floor-to-ceiling open shelving on industrial rails holds the accumulation of a family's everyday life.

The kitchen was designed around how a family actually uses it — a long oak dining table, white handleless cabinetry, stainless steel worktops, full-height crittall doors to the garden. A red perforated credenza against the whitewashed brick, topped with a brass gramophone horn, gives the room its personality in a single corner.
