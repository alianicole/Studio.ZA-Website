Creighton Ave

London 2025 Full interior design

The island is the room's argument. Substantial, unhurried, topped in a slab of Calacatta with the kind of bold gold and grey veining that only works when you commit to it fully — one continuous plane, no interruptions. Below, the cabinetry is warm walnut with integrated flush-front drawers and quietly rounded corners that keep the mass from feeling heavy. A single flush hob set into the stone. Nothing on the surface that doesn't need to be there.

Wide-plank pale oak runs underfoot and crittall-framed glazing divides the kitchen from the living space beyond, where backlit shelving anchors the far wall. The two rooms share an openness and a material seriousness — different in mood, continuous in character.
